Tool: built-in image_gen. Approved variant 1 is frame 1; approved variant 4 is frame 3. Frames 2, 4 and 5 were generated using both selected variants. Exact prompts below. Runtime loop: 5 FPS, one second.

## Shared exact prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: ONE transparent pixel-art water-ripple animation frame for an isometric RPG. Image 1 is the approved variant 1 and the edit target; image 2 is the approved variant 4, a style and animation-sequence reference. These two images are frames 1 and 3 of a five-frame looping animation at 5 FPS. Generate ONE compatible new frame, preserving their 1774x887 canvas, EXACT centered position, elliptical perspective, total footprint, calm sparse blue appearance, thickness of the main inner contact ring and transparent central hole. The contact ring must NOT change position or size between frames. Vary only small short echo-wave arcs and a few pixel-cluster glints, moving them outward by roughly 1 native game pixel as the wave travels. Add only a little detail. Fixed illumination from SCREEN UPPER LEFT ONLY; lower/right arcs darker, deep muted blue and desaturated light-blue highlights. Crisp coherent pixel clusters, readable after identical crop and nearest resize to 80x40. Genuine RGBA alpha: background, center and gaps fully transparent. NO character, opaque water surface or disk, background patch, white foam band, droplets, high splashes, glow, shadow, checkerboard, labels, text, frame or sprite sheet. Do not add a new full large ring. Keep generous padding exactly as in the references. Output a SINGLE new image.
