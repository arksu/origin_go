# Character v3: anatomical source model

The user approved a Blender character based on the existing v2 turnaround,
with production visual quality as the target. This stage covers the model,
materials and review renders; animation and game integration are later work.

Use the CC0 MakeHuman anatomical base and sculpt targets as a topology
foundation. Preserve its connected surface, UVs, facial loops and articulated
hands and feet. Adapt proportions toward the muscular young male concept.
Do not reuse the v2 ellipsoid assembly or voxel-remesh workflow.

Author chestnut swept hair, brows, detailed eyes and a draped linen loincloth.
Keep body, eyes, hair and garment meshes independently editable. Use restrained
skin variation, fabric weave, seams and hair surface detail. Store all required
dependencies locally, with upstream revision, license and checksums.

Deliver a self-contained blend file, reproducible build script/configuration,
front/side/back/three-quarter renders, and close views of face and hands.
Review actual renders after generation and correct visible problems before
delivery. Geometric checks support, but do not replace, visual review; describe
remaining quality limitations honestly.
