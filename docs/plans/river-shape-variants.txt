RIVER SHAPE VARIANTS
===================
2026-09-29 | OPTION B implemented; rendered appearance accepted by the user.

Confirmed selection
-------------------
Use OPTION B - BENDS WITHIN BENDS for natural-looking river paths.
Allow tributaries, but keep them sparse and irregularly spaced, with long
unbranched stretches. Every tributary must have a deep fairway at least
3 tiles wide, the minimum selected by the user.
Tributary frequency is independent of bend detail: more irregular curves
must not produce more branches. Current density and spacing are visually accepted.
Options A and C below remain comparisons, not additional selected features.
This selection supersedes the earlier "no variant selected" status in the
paused mapgen-living-water artifacts; their terrain plan remains rejected.

Corrected direction
-------------------
Make rivers LOOK natural, not simulate a natural drainage network.
Choose routes for player travel first; give those routes convincing shapes.
Do not generate a continent, force a coastal ocean, or drain toward borders.
Every river, including every tributary and narrow connector, must contain
a continuous deep-water fairway wide enough for boat passage.
Shallow water is optional bank decoration, never the only water in a river.

The rejected waterways implementation, its preset settings, and preview
controls have been removed. Option B extends the previous drawn-river layout,
not a drainage model. Protected footprint checks now validate final tiles;
this is not a claim of boat-runtime collision testing.
Existing unrelated terrain water is unchanged; there is no forced sea frame.

How to read the sketches
------------------------
These are deliberately schematic ASCII drawings, not generator output.
Use a monospace font with word wrap off.
A and B are the SAME two route endpoints in all three options.
Neither endpoint means source or mouth; there is no flow direction.
# traces the DEEP FAIRWAY CENTERLINE, not a one-tile-wide river.
Actual fairway width and shallow banks are separate from these path sketches.
Whitespace is land. No coast or world-center assumption is involved.

====================================
Fewer, larger bends; long readable stretches between them.
Bends vary in radius and spacing instead of repeating a sine wave.

How to build:
- Place a modest number of bend controls inside a gameplay route corridor.
- Vary bend size, spacing, and turning strength; fit a smooth curve.
- Keep endpoints fixed and preserve approach directions at junctions.
- Carve a protected deep fairway, then add banks around it.

Good: easy navigation, readable routes, relatively small implementation.
Tradeoff: alone, it can still look too smooth compared with river1.png.
Best use: calmer stretches and important long-distance travel routes.

How to build:
- Start with a route's large-scale curve, as in A.
- Add bounded medium-scale and small-scale displacements along that curve.
- Use distances along the path, not a fixed number of waves per connection.
- Sample densely enough to retain those bends without creating tile jitter.
- Limit curvature and separation so detail cannot pinch the boat fairway.
- Vary bank width gently without shrinking the protected deep core.

Good: closest proposed path character to the reference's irregular curves.
Tradeoff: unconstrained noise creates ugly wiggles and accidental joins.
Best use: the default river style, mixed with A's quieter stretches.

Important: copy the reference's visual irregularity, NOT its countless
hair-thin streams. Tributaries are occasional additions, not a dense tree.
In this game, every branch keeps boat-sized deep water through its junction.

Longer quiet reaches interrupted by distinct, deeper looping bends.

How to build:
- Compose varied bend shapes: broad arc, asymmetric S, compound bend,
  occasional open hairpin. Rotate, scale, and deform them within the route.
- Blend tangent directions where shapes meet; avoid visible template seams.
- Reserve enough land between neighboring arms of a bend.
- Reject shapes that accidentally touch, cross, or become too tight for boats.

Good: memorable locations, meaningful detours, possible portage shortcuts.
Tradeoff: overuse makes a maze; repeated templates become recognizable.
Best use: selected exploration areas, not every river segment.

WHAT WAS WRONG WITH THE PREVIOUS SHAPE CONTROLS?
================================================
Pre-change source observations that motivated Option B:
- cmd/mapgen/river.go: buildDrawPath already draws curves without elevation.
  This is the useful starting point; a hydrology replacement is unnecessary.
- A whole link gets at most 32 control segments. Long links cannot preserve
  equally detailed local bends at that cap.
- The hnh preset's shape_octave_gain is 0.06: successive noise amplitudes
  are 1, 0.06, 0.0036, ... . Fine-scale structure is strongly suppressed.
- Wavelength is derived from link length. Different-length connections
  therefore get different bend scales rather than a consistent local style.
- A sine envelope suppresses displacement toward each endpoint. Junction
  approaches need deliberate shaping rather than uniformly fading detail.

These are targeted seams for the selected Option B implementation plan.
Changing noise parameters alone is a cheap experiment, not proof of a fix.


IMPLEMENTATION OUTLINE FOR THE SELECTED DESIGN
==============================================
1. Keep the land generator and intended gameplay connections unchanged.
   Compare selected geometry on the same endpoints and seeds.
2. Implement Option B's path-shape changes in the drawn-river path.
   Do not add elevation simulation, runoff, catchments, or coastal masks.
3. Allow occasional tributaries with a separate low-density budget and
   minimum junction spacing. Do not add a branch at every bend or lake.
   Keep long unbranched reaches; tune sparse coverage in whole-map previews.
4. Carve a protected deep fairway for EVERY river role, then optional banks.
   Validate actual boat clearance after all tile/shoreline passes.
5. Review close-ups AND whole-map routes on multiple seeds before accepting.
   Check detours, sparse branch frequency, unintended crossings, and access.

The visual direction is implemented behind explicit H&H preset controls.

Rendered appearance is accepted; these ASCII sketches remain schematic.
Boat-runtime testing and global connectivity guarantees are separate concerns.
