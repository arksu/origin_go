Four ripple variants

Variants 1 and 4 were selected. Together with three new frames, they form a 5 FPS animation. View in the game renderer. Top: enlarged view; bottom: game size, 80×40, on green and blue backgrounds.

1 · Small ripples

Short waves near the inner ring with slightly uneven crest edges.

2 · Light foam

Small, sparse highlights on the crests without a continuous foam band.

3 · Drops and bubbles

Small drops and bubbles along the side arcs; the rings retain their thickness.

4 · Extra arcs

More short, broken arcs between the rings and along the outer edge.

Variants 1 and 4 were approved and included in the five-frame animation.
